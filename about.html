MEET THE FOUNDER

Humanizing technology to make emotional wellbeing accessible to all.

Nitika Nassa is the Founder & CEO of MYTRM, a mental health platform focused on Gen Z and young professionals. Her work brings together mental wellbeing, education and emotionally intelligent technology to make emotional support more accessible.

Her journey began in education and mental wellbeing, where she mentored thousands of students and worked with NCERT, Kendriya Vidyalayas and leading institutions across India. Over 4+ years of teaching and mentoring, she recognized the deep need for accessible emotional care among young adults.

At SKI Asia, she led education and sustainability initiatives and collaborated with global partners including GoSharpener, Fifty Shades Greener and Emirates Environmental Group.

The MYTRM Stated Vision

"Building personalized, emotionally intelligent companions and care networks that help people understand their emotions, behaviors, and beliefs — so everyone can connect more deeply with themselves and others."
