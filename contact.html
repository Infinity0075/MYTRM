Emergency & Crisis Support

MYTRM is an appointment-based therapy platform and is not designed for immediate crisis situations. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact Tele-MANAS (14416) or KIRAN (1800-599-0019) immediately for 24/7 free government crisis counseling in India.
